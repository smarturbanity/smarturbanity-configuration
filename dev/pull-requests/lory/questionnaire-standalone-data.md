# Keep SmartUrbanity questionnaires independent of the question pool

PlanEasy owns every question definition and its version history, including SmartUrbanity-specific questions. SmartUrbanity keeps complete questionnaire JSON with stable question references and portable editorial history; manifests load those documents without a local bank or generated bank snapshots.

This common migration preserves main's research wording. The non-survey and Marzieh survey proposals are separate branches based on this candidate. Source: lory/questionnaire-standalone-data; target: main. Validation and exact data-preservation evidence are tracked in the matching onwork note.
