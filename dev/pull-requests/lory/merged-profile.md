# Unite Your Profile and Home and work

Residents complete one profile with two sections instead of opening a second
household questionnaire. The existing 21 questions and conditional rules are
retained, with the pet question explicitly optional. The gallery then continues
directly to Your Travel. Remove the redundant household form and illustration,
and align both catalogues with the current survey inventory.

Validation: six EN/IT desktop/phone viewer paths, a phone path through the full
local Access Together page with an in-memory submission, survey bundle and
language checks, SVG checks, and six regression tests. See
[verification details](../../../docs/profile-unification.md). Completion time and
real server persistence are outside this check. Local delivery only; no push.
