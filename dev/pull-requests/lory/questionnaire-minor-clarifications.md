# Clarify non-assessable surroundings and travel reactions

People can explicitly say they cannot assess the surroundings rather than choose
an answer meaning they do not pay attention. Air-quality and weather answers no
longer combine changing route with changing time or preparing differently; a short
instruction asks for the most frequent action. Cycling barriers now include an
interrupted cycle path/lane. Changes cover all eight questionnaire languages.

Seven questions across three active surveys change. Optionality, conditions,
privacy and question count are unchanged. Split response categories receive new
values and major revisions; no historical-data compatibility is required.

Validation passed: actual-consumer survey and language verifiers, six existing
regression checks, targeted previous/current contract comparison and diff check.
Source-only delivery: app integration/browser acceptance and any dashboard closure
remain separate. This repository has no package.json or verify:pr command; CityMaaS
aggregate verification is not claimed for this configuration-only commit.
